Policies

Shipping & return policy

Plain-language terms for every order placed on this demo storefront.

Shipping & delivery

  • Delivery charge is calculated by city at checkout (Lahore Rs. 300, other major cities Rs. 800–1,500).
  • Lahore: same/next day. Other cities: 2–5 working days via insured courier.
  • 55" and above are packed in wooden crates; smaller units use double-wall cartons.
  • Order confirmation call is made before dispatch on every order.

Returns & exchange

  • Dead pixels, no-power or manufacturing fault: 7-day exchange with box and accessories.
  • Change-of-mind returns are not accepted on opened units.
  • Physical or transit damage must be reported with an unboxing video within 24 hours.
  • Wall-mounted or installed units are not returnable.

Warranty

  • 1 year panel warranty + 1 year service warranty on all demo models listed.
  • Warranty covers manufacturing defects only, not physical or liquid damage.
  • Keep the warranty card and invoice copy for service.
  • Service is handled at our Hall Road, Lahore counter.
